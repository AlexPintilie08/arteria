# Official requirements checked on 18 October 2026

Primary sources: [organizer page](https://markaiintegrator.ro/hackathon-ai/), [official regulation, Articles 7–8](https://markaiintegrator.ro/hackathon-ai/regulament-oficial.pdf), [participant guide, sections 3–4](https://markaiintegrator.ro/hackathon-ai/ghid-participanti.pdf).

The regulation limits the submitted presentation to **10 slides**, requires a functional demo/prototype link, a description of at most 300 words and a list of AI technologies/tools. It allocates three minutes to presenting and three to questions. Scoring: AI innovation/relevance 30%, real problem/impact 25%, feasibility/demo 25%, presentation 20%. Preexisting code must be disclosed. The guide reiterates the submission limit and public accessibility of links. The organizer webpage agrees on three presentation minutes, but gives 1–3 minutes for questions.

**Unresolved schedule conflict:** the regulation/guide say submission at 16:00; the webpage says 16:30–17:00 on 18 October. Neither source establishes which notice supersedes the other. Confirm with organizers rather than asserting a deadline from this audit.
